Questions

Can I learn English through Bengali?

Yes. Choose your interface language and your target language separately, so Bengali explanations can teach English.

Do I need to start at level 1?

No. A placement assessment suggests where to begin, and you can start lower if you prefer.

How is lesson accuracy checked?

Every lesson and answer key is reviewed by a person before it is published, and learners can report errors.
